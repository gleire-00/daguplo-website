Our story

Made at home, by hand, every morning

Empanada sa Bukid is run by a single mother and her two daughters from a small store beside their house. The dough is mixed before sunrise, the fillings are cooked at home, and the crimping is still done by hand.

One of the daughters also brings pre-ordered empanadas to her university on selling days, so students and teachers can reserve their pieces instead of hoping for stock.
